## Why are you leaving CRPC?
This has been on the back of my mind for a while, and although I enjoy helping keep the community safe, it is becoming extremely exhausting on not only myself but on my friends as well. 
I don't enjoy Maple County, and I don't appreciate how leadership is currently managing the community as a whole. I returned to CRPC with hopes that after what happened with Luke things would change. 
They did not. It seems as though things have only spiraled further downhill, the daily CCU for Maple County has plummeted, and with the lack of updates and care for the game it seems to only be getting worse. 
I don't see a positive future for CRPC, and I have bigger ambitions for myself as well as what I want to do with my life moving forwards.

## Where will you be?
I won't be hard to find, I am just pursuing bigger ambitions for myself while keeping the friends I made along the way as a result of CRPC. I want to help keep the Roblox platform in its entirety safer, 
and as I begin work on my Thermopylae™ project, I need to dedicate as much time as I can towards it to ensure I can deliver a product that truly achieves my vision of keeping Roblox and Discord communities 
a safer place for everyone of all ages.

## Will you come back to CRPC?
As of right now, I have no plans to return to CRPC in the near future, if the quality of the community and leadership improves I will re-consider, however for the time being I believe it is best for myself if I take a 
step back from a community I've been apart of for 3 years to achieve bigger goals. As a final send off, thank you to everyone who was apart of this adventure! It's been fun!
